The Car

A carbon-fibre rocket on four tyres

Five systems, one machine

The monocoque is a carbon survival cell the driver sits in. Everything else bolts onto it: the power unit, the aerodynamic surfaces, the suspension and the tyres.

Half engine, half battery

It pairs a 1.6-litre turbocharged V6 with an electric motor. Since 2026, the electric side delivers about half of the roughly 1,000 hp, and the fuel is 100% sustainable.

Wings that push down, not up

The wings and the floor work like upside-down aeroplane wings. The resulting downforce presses the car into the track, so it can corner far faster than grip alone would allow.

In 2026 the wings became active. They flatten on straights for speed and close up in corners for grip. DRS, the overtaking flap used from 2011 to 2025, is gone. Its replacement, Overtake Mode, gives a chasing driver extra electric power.

The halo

The halo is a titanium hoop over the cockpit, mandatory since 2018. It can carry about 12 tonnes, roughly the weight of a double-decker bus. In 2020 it saved Romain Grosjean in a fiery crash in Bahrain.

Race Craft

Softer is faster but wears out sooner

Soft (red): the most grip but the shortest life. Teams use it for qualifying and short stints.

Races are won in the pit lane

The crew changes all four tyres in about 2 seconds. The record is 1.80s.

Undercut

Pit before your rival. Fresh tyres are faster, so you can pass them while they are still in the pits.

Overcut

Stay out longer on a clear track, then pit and come out ahead.

    75 years in eight moments

    1. 1950

      The first World Championship race is held at Silverstone.

    2. 1968

      Sponsor liveries arrive. Cars stop being painted in national colours.

    3. 1977

      The Lotus 78 introduces ground effect, which uses the floor to suck the car down.

    4. 1988

      McLaren, with Senna and Prost, wins 15 of 16 races.

    5. 1994

      Senna's death at Imola sparks a safety revolution.

    6. 2014

      The hybrid era begins, with V6 turbos plus energy recovery.

    7. 2018

      The halo becomes mandatory.

    8. 2026

      A new era of active aero, a 50/50 split between combustion and electric power, sustainable fuel, and an 11th team.

    You speak paddock now.

    “Box, box”
    Come into the pits this lap.
    Dirty air
    Turbulent air behind a car that robs the follower of downforce.
    Slipstream / tow
    Following closely on a straight to get a speed boost.
    Parc fermé
    After qualifying, teams can barely touch the car.
    Lift and coast
    Lifting off early before braking to save fuel or energy.
    Graining
    Rubber balls up on the tyre surface and grip drops off.
